Optimize Gear Ratios for Vehicle Launch

Open Launch Model Open Live Script

A high launch torque improves acceleration but can amplify flexible-driveline oscillation and vehicle jerk. This workflow selects three transmission ratios that balance launch acceleration with ride comfort.

The workflow builds target velocity and acceleration profiles, estimates the required traction force and motor torque, evaluates low, medium, and high torque scenarios, and uses surrogate optimization to minimize a composite jerk-and-acceleration objective.

Quick Setup

  1. Open the workflow Live ScriptOpen Live Script

    Open optimizeVehicleLaunch.mlx to configure the launch target, vehicle parameters, ratio bounds, objective weights, and simulation budget.

  2. Define launch performanceOpen Velocity-Profile Function

    Run generateVelocityProfile to create the target velocity and acceleration and calculate the required traction and motor torque.

  3. Prepare motor torque scenariosOpen Torque-Profile Function

    Use extendedVehicleLaunchTorque.mat, or regenerate the torque profiles when the motor or inverter parameters change.

  4. Optimize and compare launch responseOpen Optimization Function

    Optimize the transmission ratios, then use simulateOptlaunch to compare the baseline and optimized launch responses.

Expected Results

The workflow produces these result types:

  • Required traction force and motor torque scenarios
  • Optimized motor, transmission, and final-drive ratios
  • Baseline and optimized jerk, acceleration, and vehicle-speed comparisons

Checks Before Using Results

  • Regenerate the torque scenarios after changing the motor, inverter, or launch target.
  • Keep the same torque scenarios, stop time, and solver settings when comparing baseline and optimized ratios.
  • Confirm each lower bound is below its corresponding upper bound and the baseline lies within the bounds.
  • Review the jerk and acceleration terms separately before accepting the composite objective.
